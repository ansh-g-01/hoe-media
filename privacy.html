Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026.

HOE Autopost is used only by the owner of House of Engineers to publish that page's own videos. It accesses the owner's YouTube account solely to upload videos to the owner's channel and read the channel name, and the owner's Instagram account solely to publish posts and read their statistics.

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data from other people is collected, stored, sold or shared. Google user data is used only to upload the owner's videos, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Contact: @hoe_houseofengineers on Instagram.
